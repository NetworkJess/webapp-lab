Task 4.1 - One-line descriptions of the files in SmartPhones
==============================================================

index.html              - Home page that links to the products page; click the
                          globe image to enter the store.

products_display.html   - The four phone products shown as <section> tags; this
                          is where the spin (onmouseover) and stop (onclick)
                          behaviour works.

products-style.css      - Styling for the store - colours, layout, and the
                          @keyframes rotation animation that makes the images
                          spin.

images/                 - Folder of product images (HTC, iPhone, Vivo, Samsung).
